Pushed by Gal's Claude, approved by Gal ("תפתח את כל האתר ותדחוף לגיט").

## What changed
- Full one-page home in `public/index.html`: floating header, hero with thermostat, brand logos, property tabs, pinned "מנוע אחד. כל הבית." story, systems bento, projects pan, process timeline with "call before the plaster" callout, testimonials, FAQ, contact form, footer, phone dock.
- New pages: `privacy/`, `accessibility/`, `404.html` (rewritten in plain Hebrew to match what the new site actually does: no tracking cookies, Netlify Forms).
- `netlify.toml`: publish `public/`, 301s from old WordPress URLs, long cache on assets.
- `noindex` on every page and `robots.txt` disallow, on purpose, until the domain moves.
- `CLAUDE.md` with team rules and structure.
- `docs/plan/` keeps the first plan and grey wireframe (Gal rejected the wireframe look; the built design replaces it).

## Why these choices
- Audience: villa and luxury-apartment owners, plus TikTok phone traffic. Gal asked for "crazy but professional, unlike Carpet DM" (Carpet DM is a playful craft site by the same team).
- The old site's real problems: broken gallery, hidden mobile form, no reasons to choose them, no WhatsApp, purple agency template.
- The story section explains VRF (the main product) without jargon, which is the main selling point for a villa owner.
